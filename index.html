Privacy Policy

App Name: Hameed Tailoring Shop

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

This app collects customer names, phone numbers, measurement data and tailoring notes that you enter manually. This data is stored locally on your device and optionally backed up to your own Google Drive account.

2. How We Use Your Information

All data entered into this app is used solely for managing tailoring records. We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.

3. Google Drive Integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive, this app uses the Google Drive API only to backup and restore your tailoring database file. The app only accesses files it creates itself inside your Google Drive. We do not access any other files in your Google Drive.

4. Google Account Information

When you sign in with Google, this app reads your name, email address and profile photo only to display your account information inside the app. This information is never stored on any server or shared with anyone.

5. Data Storage

All tailoring records are stored locally on your device in a SQLite database file. Backup files are stored only in your own Google Drive account and are never accessible to us.

6. Data Security

Your data never leaves your device except when you voluntarily choose to backup to your own Google Drive. We have no servers and do not collect any data.

7. Children Privacy

This app is designed for business use by tailoring shop owners. It is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy please contact us at your Gmail address.
